# Surface brief: Archive (+ Reading Desk)

Scope: public paper catalog wall and single-paper reading desk.
Mode: Read (catalog scanning) + Operate (open, download, jump).

Audience/job: any visitor finds a paper and studies it; admin additionally edits classification and figures.
Proof/content: real paper rows, question counts, type chips, TeX renders.
Constraints: students see done papers only; edit UI never renders for students; index collapses to select past 40 questions; reading pane max 75ch; figures capped 400px with text provenance.

Direction: catalog wall of hairline cards (serif title, mono ledger line, chips, source rows, Open primary); reading desk header with Display title + ledger + Prev/Next/Jump bar, min 44px targets.
Memorable moment: the reading desk header - serif paper title over its mono ledger line.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The archive is a catalog, not a dashboard; one column of quiet cards earns trust, metrics stay in the back office.
OWN-WORLD: Paper ground, hairline frames, serif titles, mono ledger lines, Seal Red only on Open and the active question marker.
STORY: Visitor scans titles, opens a paper, reads one question at a time with solutions on demand.
FIRST VIEWPORT: H1 Archive, search + exam filter row, first catalog cards fully visible with Open actions.
FORM: single-column card wall (established Streamlit list, elevated to custom HTML cards).
SEED REFERENCE: seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
