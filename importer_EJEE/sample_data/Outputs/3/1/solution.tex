\underline{\textbf{Solution for 1}}

$a = 2t - 6$

$dv = a \, dt$

$\int_{0}^{v} dv = \int_{0}^{t} (2t - 6) \, dt$

$v = t^2 - 6t$

$ds = v \, dt$

$\int_{0}^{s} ds = \int_{0}^{t} (t^2 - 6t) \, dt$

$s = \dfrac{t^3}{3} - 3t^2$

When $t = 6 \, \text{s}$,

$v = 0$

When $t = 11 \, \text{s}$,

$s = 80.7 \, \text{m}$
